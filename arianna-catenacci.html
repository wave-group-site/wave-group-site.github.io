Current research

My research focuses on the mechanistic understanding of anaerobic bioprocesses for energy (biogas and biomethane) and carbon recovery (short- and medium-chain fatty acids), using mathematical modelling at different scales, from metabolic to reactor scale, for process analysis and understanding, together with experimental methods tailored to the specific needs of model validation.
