Ali Amini

Google Scholar Contact the group
Specialty

Purple phototrophic bacteria, from kinetics to reactor scale-up

Ali Amini is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the WAVE group at DICA, Politecnico di Milano. His research centers on purple phototrophic bacteria (PPB) mixed cultures for wastewater treatment and resource recovery — from biokinetic modelling of light-driven growth to the design and scale-up of flat-plate photobioreactors.

He works at the intersection of experimental microbiology and mechanistic modelling, translating lab-scale photobioreactor behavior into scalable, circular-bioeconomy processes: recovering single-cell protein from agro-food by-products like cheese whey, and converting PPB biomass into agricultural biostimulants.
