Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains, in plain terms, what this website (wave-group-site.github.io) — the site of the WAVE (Waste to Value Engineering) research group at DICA, Politecnico di Milano — stores in your browser, and why.

What this site does not do

This is a static, informational website with no user accounts, no e-commerce, and no advertising. We do not run analytics or tracking scripts, we do not use advertising or profiling cookies, and we do not sell or share visitor data with third parties.

What we store in your browser

The site uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember two simple preferences, purely to make your next visit smoother:

KeyPurposeDuration
wave-lang Remembers whether you last viewed the site in English or Italian, so the language is applied automatically on your next visit. Until you clear your browser data
wave-cookie-consent Remembers your choices from the cookie notice (see categories below), so it does not reappear on every page. Until you clear your browser data

This information stays in your browser only — it is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it.

Cookie categories

The cookie notice groups storage into standard categories for clarity. On this site, only Necessary is actually in use — the others are shown for transparency but currently hold nothing:

CategoryStatus on this site
NecessaryIn use — language preference and consent choice. Cannot be disabled.
FunctionalNot used.
PerformanceNot used.
AnalyticsNot used — no analytics service is installed on this site.
OthersNot used.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub's servers may process standard technical information (such as your IP address) purely to deliver the pages to your browser, under GitHub's own privacy practices. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Managing or clearing your preferences

You can clear the stored preferences at any time from your browser's settings (site data / local storage for this domain). Doing so will reset the language to your browser's default and show the cookie notice again on your next visit.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to andrea.turolla@polimi.it or elena.ficara@polimi.it.
