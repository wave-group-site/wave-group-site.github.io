Research

Four angles on the same problem

Our projects sit at the intersection of microbiology, photochemistry, and process engineering, all aimed at closing the loop between what a treatment plant takes in and what it can give back.

Biological & microalgae-based treatment

Engineering microbial and microalgae-bacteria consortia that remove organic load and nutrients more reliably, using less energy than conventional activated sludge.

Nutrient & resource recovery

Capturing nitrogen and phosphorus from wastewater and sewage sludge and converting them into usable fertilizer products instead of discharge byproducts.

Advanced oxidation & disinfection

Developing photocatalytic and advanced oxidation processes to disinfect water, remove emerging contaminants, and enable safe wastewater reuse.

Process modeling & scale-up

Translating lab-scale results into models and pilot designs that utilities and industry partners can actually implement.

Approach

From waste stream to value stream

Every project in the group follows the same underlying logic, applied to a different waste type or process.

01

Characterize the waste

Understand the composition, variability, and hidden potential of a wastewater or byproduct stream.

02

Engineer the process

Design biological or advanced oxidation systems that selectively remove, transform, or recover target compounds.

03

Recover the value

Deliver a usable output — clean water, fertilizer, chemicals, or metals — at a scale that makes sense for real facilities.
